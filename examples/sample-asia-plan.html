Net VEX 725 per vol point, unchanged from 15:22 — the same 2026-09-23 final book. QN4U6 holds 52.4; Wednesday's QNEU6 holds the most of any single expiry, 82.1. This book is a day behind the tape: it cannot see Thursday's range. Tonight's 09-24 preliminary lands around 19:00 PT; the 0DTE figures above are the ones most likely to move.
Method. Three books on one axis: 0DTE is the contract expiring today, Near is every expiry to 9 DTE, Full is all listed expiries (the ⬤ score alone uses three disjoint books: 0DTE, 1–9 DTE, 10+ DTE). Open interest and settlement prices come from Databento GLBX.MDP3 (CME's licensed redistributor) for the stated settle date. Implied vol is solved per strike from those settlement prices, so the smile is measured rather than borrowed and nothing is integer-rounded — the gamma column is computed under Black-76 at each contract's actual time to expiry, with each expiry priced against its own underlying future so the NQU6/NQZ6 basis is preserved. Bars are net gamma, call minus put, and each column is scaled to its own maximum, so lengths compare within a column and not across. Flips are the futures price where Σ Γᵢ(F)·(OI_call,i − OI_put,i) crosses zero, reported against the reference future's own settle. VEX is Σ vanna·(OI_call,i − OI_put,i) per vol point over the full book. Time is counted in business time — sessions, variance-weighted, weekends and holidays zero — for the vol solve and every Greek alike, so the one-session rails S × ATM IV ÷ √252 are consistent with the vol they use. Books settling into a different future (NQZ6 and later) are basis-shifted onto the reference axis before they are plotted, so a strike lands where its hedging flow reaches this contract's tape. Γ near price is the same net gamma summed only over strikes inside the band actually drawn (±1 expected move for a full day, scaled down for a part-session) around the live price — the chain-wide totals are a property of the book alone and so do not move when price does, while this one does, which is what makes it the figure to watch across rebuilds on an unchanged book.

Caveats. The dealer convention — long calls, short puts — is an assumption about positioning, not an observation; every sign here inverts if it is wrong. The open interest is the prior settle, so the current session's own build is missing, which matters most for the 0DTE book. The confluence dots describe which of three disjoint books (0DTE / 1–9 DTE / 10+ DTE) a strike appears in, not a ranking, and the setups above are ordered by gamma structure rather than by that score. This is positioning analysis, not advice.
